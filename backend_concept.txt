🗄️ Core Database Architecture (PostgreSQL)
1. Users Table (Authentication & Roles)

user_id (Primary Key)

email, password_hash

role (Enum: 'FARM_MANAGER', 'SUPERMARKET_BUYER', 'LOGISTICS_ADMIN')

2. Locations Table (The Geocoding Hub)

location_id (Primary Key)

address_text (e.g., "Jalan SS 15/4, Subang Jaya")

latitude (Float)

longitude (Float)

region_state (String - used for the AI Milk Run grouping)

3. Farms & Customers Tables (The Profiles)

farm_id (Linked to Users and Locations)

daily_capacity_boxes (Integer)

customer_id (Linked to Users and Locations)

4. Orders Table (The Live Marketplace)

order_id (Primary Key)

customer_id (Foreign Key)

sku_id (Vegetable type)

quantity_boxes (Integer)

requires_cold_chain (Boolean: True/False)

max_lead_time_hours (Integer)

status (Enum: 'PENDING', 'AI_PROCESSING', 'DISPATCHED', 'DELIVERED')

5. Milk_Runs Table (The AI Output / The Master Plan)

This is where your Python AI saves its answers!

route_id (Primary Key)

farm_id (Origin)

assigned_vehicle (e.g., 'LORRY_AMB_500')

total_boxes_loaded (Integer)

optimized_distance_km (Float - from your TSP algorithm)

total_route_cost_rm (Float)

How the App Actually Works (The User Flow)
Morning (9:00 AM): Supermarkets log in and submit rows into the Orders table. Status is PENDING.

Cut-off Time (12:00 PM): The system locks new orders.

The AI Trigger: Your FastAPI backend wakes up. It grabs all PENDING orders, groups them by State, runs the Nearest Neighbor TSP algorithm, and figures out the trucks.

The Output: The AI creates new rows in the Milk_Runs table and updates the orders to DISPATCHED.

Afternoon (1:00 PM): The Logistics Admin opens the dashboard, sees the beautiful Streamlit/React map with the exact routes drawn out, and sends the trucks on their way.
